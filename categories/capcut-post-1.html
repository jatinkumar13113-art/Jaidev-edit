What is a CapCut Template?

A CapCut template is a ready-made video format that includes transitions, effects, filters, and music. You just add your photos or videos and export.

How to Use CapCut Templates

  1. Click on the Use Template button
  2. It will open in the CapCut app
  3. Add your photos or videos
  4. Preview and export
